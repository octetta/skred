Benchmark Overview:
ma-bench is an audio performance diagnostic tool designed to measure real-time callback stability in miniaudio. It executes a synthetic sine wave synthesis task across various sample rates and buffer sizes to identify the operational limits of the audio hardware and driver stack.

Metrics Explained:
* exec (ms): The actual time taken by the audio_callback to generate one buffer of audio. A value approaching the period_ms indicates the system is nearing the processing limit.
* jitter (ms): The variation in the execution time between consecutive callbacks. Lower jitter indicates a more stable, predictable audio thread.
* cpu (%): The relative CPU utilization monitored during the test duration.
* underruns: A count of instances where the audio callback was invoked later than the expected buffer period. High underrun counts manifest as audible clicks, pops, or "crackling."
